FAQ

1. Why do I need to create an account?

Answer: Creating an account allows you to save your preferences, view your history, track orders, and securely access features exclusive to registered users.

2. What should I do if I didn't receive the verification/confirmation email?

Answer: First, check your spam or junk folder. If it isn't there, wait a few minutes and request a new verification link using the "Resend Email" button on the sign-in page. Make sure your email address was typed correctly.

3. What are the requirements for creating a password?

Answer: To keep your account secure, your password must usually be at least 8 characters long and include a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and at least one special character (e.g., @, #, !).

4. How will my personal information and email address be used?

Answer: We strictly protect your privacy. Your information is used only to manage your account and provide services to you. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.

5. What if I already have an account or forgot my password?

Answer: If you already have an account, click the Sign In link instead of registering. If you forgot your password, click "Forgot Password?" on the login page to reset it via email.
